Taskora Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 July 2026
Operator: Nomadic Notions (www.nomadic-notions.co.in)
Contact: taskora-support@nomadic-notions.co.in

This Privacy Policy describes how Taskora (“the App”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Taskora mobile and web applications. By using Taskora, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Taskora is a personal task-planning application operated by Nomadic Notions. Support requests can be sent to taskora-support@nomadic-notions.co.in.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may process:

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, Microsoft, or Apple (iOS), we receive identifiers such as your name and email address from that provider. We do not receive or store your OAuth provider password.
  • Task and planning data. Tasks, themes, preferences, and related planning content you create. This data is stored on your device for offline use and synced to our servers so it is available across your devices.
  • Authentication tokens. Session tokens stored securely on your device (for example via the platform secure store) so you stay signed in.
  • Device and app settings. Optional biometric unlock preference, reminder preferences, and (on supported devices) a push notification token so we can deliver local or remote reminders you enable.
  • Microphone / speech (optional). If you use AudioTask / voice capture, the App requests microphone and speech-recognition permission to convert speech into task text. Audio is processed to create the task; we do not sell audio recordings.
  • Product analytics (when enabled). We may use PostHog to collect aggregated product events (for example screen views, login success/failure without passwords, task create/complete counts, sync outcomes, and app version/platform). Events are configured not to include task titles, notes, transcripts, or email addresses.

3. How we use information

  • Provide, sync, and improve Taskora’s core planning features
  • Authenticate you and secure your account session
  • Send reminders you opt into
  • Diagnose reliability issues and understand product usage at an aggregate level
  • Respond to support requests you send us

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Where data is stored and who processes it

  • On your device: local task database (offline), secure tokens, and preferences.
  • Our API and database: Taskora’s backend runs on Cloudflare Workers with data stored in a managed Postgres database (Neon) so your synced tasks remain available across devices.
  • Sign-in providers: Google, Microsoft, and/or Apple process authentication according to their own policies when you choose those sign-in methods.
  • Analytics: PostHog (when configured) processes product analytics events on our behalf.
  • Hosting: the Taskora web client may be hosted on Vercel; native apps are distributed via Google Play and (when available) the Apple App Store.

5. Legal bases (where applicable)

Where required (for example under GDPR), we process data to perform our contract with you (providing the App), based on your consent (for example microphone or optional analytics where consent is required), and for legitimate interests such as securing and improving the service in ways that do not override your rights.

6. Retention

We retain account and synced task data while your account is active. You may delete tasks in the App. If you want your account and associated server data deleted, contact taskora-support@nomadic-notions.co.in and we will process the request within a reasonable time. Local data can also be removed by uninstalling the App or clearing site data on web.

7. Security

We use industry-standard transport encryption (HTTPS), store session credentials in platform-secure storage where available, and isolate each user’s task data by account. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please use a trusted device and keep your OAuth account secured.

8. Children’s privacy

Taskora is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal data, or to object to / restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, email taskora-support@nomadic-notions.co.in. You may also revoke OAuth access from your Google, Microsoft, or Apple account settings.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours (including the United States and European Economic Area). Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards offered by those providers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Taskora after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy where permitted by law.
