details: >-
  Walking Challenge is a minimal wellness app for small private groups who want friendly competition around daily steps. Create or join a group with an invite code (up to five members), sync activity from Health Connect on Android, and see how you rank on daily, weekly, monthly, and rolling 90-day leaderboards. On Android, steps sync from your health app so progress stays accurate without manual entry. On web, check scores, manage your profile, and stay connected   when you are away from your phone. Organizers can approve joins, moderate the group, and keep challenges private — no public feed or stranger discovery. Built for friends, families, and workplace step challenges. Sign in with Google or Apple, sync your progress, and compete kindly across web and Android.
