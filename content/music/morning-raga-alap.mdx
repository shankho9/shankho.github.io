## About this piece

A slow **alap** introduction suitable for morning practice. Focus on breath control and long sustained notes.

### Structure

1. **Alap** — free rhythm, exploring the raga scale
2. **Jor** — gentle pulse emerges
3. **Jhala** — faster plucked section (optional)

### Practice notes

- Begin in **lower register**, gradually ascend
- Leave space between phrases — silence is part of the music
- Record yourself and compare intonation week over week
