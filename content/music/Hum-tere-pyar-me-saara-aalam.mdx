\:: Panchhee se chhudaakar us kaa ghar,

tum apane ghar par le aaye,

ye pyaar kaa pinjaraa man bhaayaa,

hum jee bhar bhar kar musakaye,

jab pyaar huaa is pinjade se,

tum kahane lage aazaad raho,

hum kaise bhulaaye pyaar teraa,

tum apanee jubaan se ye naa kaho,

ab tum saa jahaan mein koee nahin hain,

hum to tumhaare ho baithhe,

tum kahate ho ke ayese pyaar ko

bhool jaao, bhool jaao

Hum tere pyaar mein saaraa aalam

\:: Is tere charan kee dhool se hum

ne apanee jeewan maang bharee

jab hi to suhaagan kahalaayee,

duniyaan kee najar mein pyaar banee

tum pyaar kee sundar moorat ho,

aur pyaar humaaree poojaa hai,

ab in charanon mein dam nikale,

bas itanee aaur tamannaa hai,

hum pyaar ke gangaajal se balam ji

tanaman apanaa dho baithhe

tum kahate ho ke ayese pyaar ko

bhool jaao, bhool jaao

Hum tere pyaar mein saaraa aalam

\:: sapanon kaa darpan dekhaa thaa,

sapanon kaa darpan tod diyaa,

ye pyaar kaa aanchal humane to,

daaman se tumhaare baandh liyaa,

ye ayesee gaanthh hain ulafata kee,

jis ko naa koee bhee khol sakaa,

tum aan base jab is dil me,

dil fir to kahee naa dol sakaa,

o pyaar ke saagar hum teree

laharon mein naaw doobo baithhe

tum kahate ho ke ayese pyaar

ko bhool jaao, bhool jaao

\:: Hum tere pyaar mein saaraa aalam,

kho baithhe hain, kho baithhe

tum kahate ho ke ayese pyaar ko

bhool jaao, bhool jaao

Hum tere pyaar mein saaraa aalam.
