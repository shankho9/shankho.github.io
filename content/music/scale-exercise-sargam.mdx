## Basic sargam exercise

Use this as a daily warm-up. Each line should be sung or played slowly, then gradually increase tempo.

```
Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni Sa
Sa Ni Dha Pa Ma Ga Re Sa
```

### Finger positions (keyboard reference)

| Note | Western | Key (C major) |
| ---- | ------- | ------------- |
| Sa   | C       | C             |
| Re   | D       | D             |
| Ga   | E       | E             |
| Ma   | F       | F             |

Repeat each pattern **four times** before moving to the next.
